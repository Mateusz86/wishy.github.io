Privacy Policy for Wishy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

The Wishy mobile application is developed and operated by Mateusz Drożdż. Wishy is designed as a wishlist management and toy budget planning tool for parents and guardians. This Privacy Policy explains how user information is handled within the app.

1. Information Collection and Use

Wishy is built with privacy as a core priority:

  • User-Entered Data: All information created within the app—including wishlists, gift ideas, item prices, budgets, and names—is stored exclusively on your local device. None of this data is transmitted to or stored on external servers.
  • Tracking and Analytics: The app does not collect sensitive tracking data, user identifiers, or personal location data in the background.

2. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any user data to third parties. Because all app data remains strictly on your local device, the developer has no access to your personal information.

3. Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in operating system security features. We recommend using standard device security (passcode, PIN, or biometrics) to secure access to your phone and local application data.

4. Children's Privacy

Although Wishy handles gift and toy wishlists, the application is intended strictly for use by parents, legal guardians, and adults (18 years of age and older). The app does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time if new app features require it. Any updates will be published directly on this page.
